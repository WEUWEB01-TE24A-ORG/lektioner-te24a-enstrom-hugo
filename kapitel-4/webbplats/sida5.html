LASH

Jacob Lash is an asshole Hero who eagerly initiates fights with grappling hooks and whips, chasing down fleeing opponents with glee.

He leaps into combat with his Grappling hook, Flogging his foes with a life-stealing whip and stomping the ground with a Ground Strike that damages multiple targets. If the enemy team groups up, the Lash can pick them all up at once and Death Slam them into the ground.
